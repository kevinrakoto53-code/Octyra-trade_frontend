Lesson 1

What is RSI?

The Relative Strength Index measures momentum. Above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold.

💡 Use RSI to spot reversals before they happen.

Lesson 2

MACD Explained

MACD shows the relationship between two moving averages. A crossover = potential trend change.

💡 Combine MACD with volume for stronger signals.

Lesson 3

Bollinger Bands

Bands that expand and contract with volatility. Price touching the band = potential reversal.

💡 Squeeze = big move coming. Watch carefully.

Lesson 4

Risk Management

Never risk more than 2% per trade. Use stop-loss orders to protect your capital always.

💡 The goal is to stay in the game, not win every trade.

Lesson 5

Support & Resistance

Key price levels where buying or selling pressure is strong. Break = continuation signal.

💡 These levels work because everyone watches them.

Lesson 6

Candlestick Patterns

Doji, Hammer, Engulfing — each candle tells a story about buyer vs seller battles.

💡 Master 5 patterns and you'll have an edge.
